Michigan's short winter days mean your outdoor lighting works overtime. A well-designed landscape lighting system doesn't just look stunning — it extends your usable outdoor time and adds real security to your property.

Here are seven lighting ideas we install most often for homeowners in Oakland and Macomb Counties.

## 1. Path Lighting Along Walkways

The most practical upgrade you can make. Low-profile path lights along your front walk, driveway edges, and garden paths provide safe footing after dark while creating a welcoming glow.

**Pro tip:** Space fixtures 6–8 feet apart and stagger them on alternating sides for the most natural look.

## 2. Architectural Uplighting

Aiming lights upward at your home's facade, columns, or stonework creates dramatic shadows and highlights architectural features. This is the single highest-impact lighting technique for curb appeal.

We recommend **warm white (2700K)** LED fixtures for a cozy, inviting look that complements Michigan's residential architecture.

## 3. Tree Uplighting and Moonlighting

Large trees are a major asset in Michigan landscapes. Uplighting mature oaks, maples, and evergreens from below showcases their canopy structure — especially beautiful in winter when branches are bare.

**Moonlighting** takes the opposite approach: mounting fixtures high in the tree and angling down to create dappled, natural-looking light on the ground below.

## 4. Deck and Patio Step Lights

Recessed step lights built into your deck stairs, patio walls, or retaining wall caps serve double duty: they prevent trips and falls while adding a sophisticated finished look.

## 5. Hardscape Wall Wash

If you've invested in a beautiful stone retaining wall or paver patio, wall-wash lighting shows off the texture and craftsmanship. Linear LED strips tucked under cap stones or along wall bases create a clean, modern effect.

## 6. Water Feature Lighting

Underwater and perimeter lighting around fountains, ponds, or waterfalls turns these features into nighttime focal points. Submersible LED fixtures are energy-efficient and rated for year-round Michigan conditions.

## 7. Security and Accent Lighting Combined

Modern landscape lighting systems can integrate motion-activated zones with permanent accent lighting. Your front yard looks beautiful every night, but motion sensors near side yards and back entries add a security layer without the harsh floodlight look.

## LED vs. Halogen: The Michigan Choice

We exclusively install **LED landscape lighting** for our clients. Here's why:

- **90% less energy** than halogen
- **50,000+ hour lifespan** (vs. 2,000 for halogen bulbs)
- No heat buildup near plants
- Consistent color output in cold temperatures

Michigan's cold winters actually favor LEDs — they perform better in low temperatures than halogen fixtures.
