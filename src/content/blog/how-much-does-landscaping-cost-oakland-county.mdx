One of the first questions homeowners ask is "how much will this cost?" It's a fair question, and we believe in transparent pricing. While every project is different, here are the typical ranges we see for landscaping work in Oakland County, Michigan.

## Lawn Care & Maintenance

| Service | Typical Cost |
|---------|-------------|
| Weekly mowing (avg. lot) | $40 – $65/visit |
| Spring/fall cleanup | $200 – $400 |
| Fertilizer program (6 apps) | $350 – $550/season |
| Aeration + overseeding | $200 – $350 |
| Full maintenance package | $2,500 – $4,500/year |

These prices reflect lots in the Rochester Hills, Troy, and Shelby Township area. Larger properties or steep grades may cost more.

## Landscape Design & Planting

| Project | Typical Cost |
|---------|-------------|
| Design consultation | Free – $500 |
| Foundation planting (front only) | $2,000 – $5,000 |
| Full landscape renovation | $8,000 – $25,000+ |
| Mature tree planting | $500 – $2,000/tree |
| Privacy hedge installation | $1,500 – $4,000 |

The biggest variable is plant size. A 3-gallon shrub costs $35–$60 installed, while a 7-gallon specimen is $80–$150+.

## Hardscape Installation

| Project | Typical Cost |
|---------|-------------|
| Paver patio (200–400 sq ft) | $5,000 – $15,000 |
| Paver driveway | $15,000 – $35,000 |
| Retaining wall (per linear ft) | $50 – $150 |
| Outdoor kitchen | $15,000 – $50,000+ |
| Fire pit (built-in) | $3,000 – $8,000 |
| Walkway (front entry) | $3,000 – $8,000 |

Material choice drives the price. Standard concrete pavers are the most affordable, while natural stone (bluestone, travertine) costs 2–3x more.

## What Affects Your Price

Several factors influence where your project falls in these ranges:

1. **Site access** — tight lots or steep grades require more labor
2. **Soil conditions** — clay-heavy soil (common in Macomb County) requires extra drainage work
3. **Material selection** — premium materials add 30–50% to base pricing
4. **Project timing** — spring and early summer are peak season; fall projects may offer better availability
5. **Scope** — bundling multiple services (patio + lighting + planting) often provides better overall value
